# WalnutIQ: Tree Care & AI Count

**WalnutIQ: Tree Care & AI Count** (*Walnut Tree Care & AI Counter*) is an intelligent orchard companion app and web platform built for walnut growers, home orchardists, and agricultural researchers.

Designed with a high-contrast, sunlight-readable **Light Walnut Green** interface, WalnutIQ combines year-round agronomic tree care with on-device computer vision and smart orchard sensor telemetry.

---

## Key Features

### 1. AI Walnut Canopy Counter & Yield Estimator
- Point your phone camera at the tree canopy across 4 quadrants (North, East, South, West) to automatically detect and count developing walnuts in the leaves.
- Calculates whole-tree yield estimates (total nut count and estimated harvest weight in `kg`) adjusted for canopy density and leaf occlusion.

### 2. Post-Harvest Ground Walnut Finder
- Sweep your camera over soil, tall grass, and fallen autumn leaves after shaking the tree.
- Highlights camouflaged fallen walnuts (both clean **in-shell** walnuts and nuts still inside **splitting green/dark hulls**) with high-visibility AR bounding boxes so no harvest is left behind.

### 3. Year-Round Walnut Care Calendar & Cycle Countdowns
- Guided annual care plans covering **Pruning, Watering, NPK & Micronutrient (Zinc/Boron) Feeding, Blight & Husk Fly Prevention, Harvesting, and Winter Dormancy Care**.
- Interactive **Daily & Weekly Guidance Popups** with live countdown timers for irrigation intervals, fertilizer windows, hull-split readiness, and winter chill hours (`< 7°C`).

### 4. Tuya Smart Orchard Sensor Integration
- Live front-screen telemetry and threshold alerts for **Soil Moisture, Soil & Air Temperature, Weather & Rain Forecast, Sunlight / UV Intensity (Lux), and Frost Watch**.

### 5. Offline-First Field Reliability + Cloud Sync
- Works **100% offline** in remote walnut groves with zero cellular reception.
- Automatically syncs tree scans, sensor snapshots, and care logs to the cloud when back online, allowing seamless access across mobile devices and the companion web portal (**walnut.pinarlik.com**).
- Built-in **14-day local storage auto-purge** removes old cached scan files from the phone once they are safely backed up to the cloud.

### 6. Grower Community & Shareable Tree Scan Cards
- Real-time channels for walnut growers to discuss pruning training systems, irrigation schedules, pest diagnostics, and share interactive **Tree Scan & Yield Cards**.
